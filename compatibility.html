Can I Donate Blood Today?

Answer 4 quick questions to check your basic eligibility.

1. Are you between 18 and 65 years of age?
2. Do you weigh at least 50 kg (110 lbs)?
3. Are you feeling healthy, well, and free of acute infections today?
4. Has it been at least 3 months (men) or 4 months (women) since your last donation?
